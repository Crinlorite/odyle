## What people say

There are two versions:

- **Four slots and a cap at level 20.** That's what a guide built on the global client says: the first one opens at level 22 and the rest at 27, 32 and 37.
- **Six slots and a cap at level 25.** That's what Korea has had since March and July 2026, and what the guides that draw from there repeat.

## What happens

It's four. At level 26, my Gladiator's stigma tab shows 13 stigmas, not 18, and the bar has four slots for them.

Of the 13, I had one unlocked, Wrath Wave, and the other twelve locked. A stigma's upgrades kick in at levels 5, 10, 15 and 20, which fits the cap at 20. Raising Wrath Wave from 1 to 2 costs one shard.

What I haven't seen yet is at what level each slot opens: the guides say 27, 32 and 37 for the three I'm missing.

## What to do

Plan around four slots and 13 stigmas. If a guide recommends a stigma you can't find in your list, it's from the Korean version.

Shards are scarce while you level, so don't spread them out: pick the stigmas you're going to run and level those.
