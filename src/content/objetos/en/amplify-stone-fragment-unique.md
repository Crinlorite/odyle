## What it's for

Just one thing: combining them in Substance Morph (“Conversión” in the Spanish client) to get an [Amplify Stone (Unique)](/en/objetos/amplify-stone-unique/). According to wikily's recipe table, each stone takes **4 fragments and 2,500 Enhance Stones**. I haven't seen it in my own Substance Morph yet.

## Where it drops

**Seen in game:** Ascension Trial on Normal gave me **9** on 12 October and **8** on the 14th. The three weekly attempts give 24 to 27: just enough for the first amplification step on one piece, which takes 6 stones ([the maths](/en/apuntes/monedas-materiales-y-contenidos/)).

**According to metabot:**

- The Abyssal Forge: Ludra boss always drops it.
- Fire Temple cubes, almost half the time (48.8%).
- The Deus Research Base bosses, one time in five.
- The Trade Shop and the Season Shop sell it for 500 Phantasmal Fragment.
