## What it's for

When a **gold** piece (Unique grade) reaches its max +, [Enhance Stones](/en/objetos/enhance-stone/) are no longer enough: to keep going you have to **amplify** it, and every step uses these stones. The guides say the normal max is +15 and that amplification takes you to +20. Metabot counts 354 pieces that can be amplified and says each step takes up to 20 stones.

**Blue** pieces can't be amplified. In my game they stayed at +10 and went no further.

## Where it comes from

**According to metabot**, only from Substance Morph, by combining [fragments](/en/objetos/amplify-stone-fragment-unique/). Since this unbound version is tradable, you can also buy it on the market.
