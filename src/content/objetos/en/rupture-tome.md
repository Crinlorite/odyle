## What it's for

It's an item level 54 weapon for the **Sorcerer**. If it drops for you on another class, as it did for me on my Gladiator, don't throw it away.

**Seen in game:** it works as **Sync material** in Soul Binding. With it I changed lines on my pieces, and every new line came out at its maximum value. It can't be sold on the Market because it binds on pickup. The NPC pays 30,000 Kina.

When a piece reaches 100% Soul Binding, the next step is Soul Fuse, which needs [Philosopher's Stone: Revelation](/en/objetos/philosophers-stone-revelation/).

The [Rupture Rod](/en/objetos/rupture-rod/) is the same weapon for the Chanter and works the same way.

## Where it drops

**According to metabot:**

- From the boss **Ultimate Berk** in Krao Cave, at a very low chance (0.43%).
- From the Krao Cave weapon selection chest.
- From the Abyssal Forge: Ludra boss (1.49%).

Enhancing it is safe up to +10. The step to +11 succeeds 65% of the time, and each failure raises the chance of the next attempt. It goes up to +15.
