## What it's for

Every attempt to raise a piece's + spends Enhance Stones, and the higher the +, the more it asks for. Metabot counts almost 2,000 items that use it. It can't be sold or moved to another character.

Once a golden piece hits its cap, what comes next isn't this anymore: it's [Amplify Stone (Unique)](/en/objetos/amplify-stone-unique/).

## Where it drops

**Seen in game, on 12 October:**

- Ascension Trial on Normal: 1,500 per run.
- Bio-Research at 1,536 item level: 6,700.

**According to metabot**, the bosses of Deus Research Base, Draupnir and Ferocious Horn Den also always drop it, and it comes from quests, achievements and fissure chests.
