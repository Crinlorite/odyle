## What people say

Korean and German guides say Daevanion raises item level “one by one”. None of them clarifies whether a stat node counts the same as a skill node, or what happens with Azphel, the PvP board, which has its own crystal counter.

## What happens

I measured it spending only crystals, without touching anything else in between:

| Crystals | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 12, in Zikel and Nezekan | 941 | 953 |
| 7, in Zikel and Nezekan | 1,285 | 1,292 |
| 2 Azphel | 1,376 | 1,378 |

Always one point per crystal, whatever the node. Azphel ones count too, even though their nodes are health, mana or PvP.

## What to do

- Don't leave any crystal unspent: each one is item level.
- The Nightmare shop sells regular crystals at 300 fragments, 50 per character. Every good Nightmare run gives enough for several.
- The Abyss shop sells Azphel crystals at 10,000 Abyss Points, 20 per character. They also work for raising item level, even if they add little to combat power.
