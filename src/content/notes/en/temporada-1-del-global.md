## Which version it is

The global version starts a year behind Korea in content, but with that year's balance changes already applied. That's why many Korean guides talk about things that don't exist here.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Season 1 | 30 September to 16 December 2026 |
| Season 2 | Starts 16 December 2026 |
| Weekly reset | Wednesday at 09:00, server time |
| Level cap | 45 |
| Groups and raids | 5 and 10 players |

## What Season 1 doesn't have

| Korea today | Global |
|---|---|
| 9 classes | 8, no Brawler |
| 18 stigmas and 6 slots | 13 stigmas and 4 slots |
| 8 Daevanion boards | 5: Nezekan, Zikel, Vaizel, Triniel and Azphel; Ariel since 21 October |
| 7 Arcana sets | 2 |
| Red gear | None, according to a Taiwanese player who tried the Steam version |
| PvP gear with stats | Removed; it comes back as appearances |

The Ariel board, which Korean guides tell you to start first, reached the global client with the [21 October patch](/en/apuntes/parche-21-oct-expediciones-hard/). Until then it did not exist and its crystal was useless; now it has a use, but it only gives PvE stats.

## Servers

Each server belongs to a single faction, and each one is paired with a server of the opposite faction as its rival. NCSOFT publishes the pairings and they change every so often.

- In the official list of 6 October 2026, the European Elyos server Nezekan is paired with the Asmodian Zikel.
- Transfers between servers of the same faction start on 14 October 2026 and are free: one transfer per account, with as many characters as you want from that server. The notice sets restrictions between early access servers and launch servers: read it before counting on it. What is known about the dates is in [the note on the 14 October patch](/en/apuntes/parche-14-oct-y-halloween/).
- Dungeons and the rest of the instanced content are played cross-server.

Outages are announced in the AION 2 news on Steam and on the official board. The return isn't always: on 8 October the servers came back without the notice changing, so the safe bet is to try logging in. The ones so far:

| Date | What happened | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 7 October 2026 | Maintenance with a patch, from 08:30 (Spanish time) | 3 h 30 min in Europe; 5 h 30 min in Asia |
| 8 October 2026 | Outage of the cloud service NCSOFT uses, on the 22 European servers of both factions. Notice at 12:43 (Spanish time) | Over two hours and twenty minutes: by 15:07 you could log in to Nezekan. NCSOFT didn't announce the return |
| 14 October 2026 | Maintenance with a patch and the Halloween event, from 08:30 (Spanish time). [What it brings](/en/apuntes/parche-14-oct-y-halloween/) | 4 h planned |

## Getting to 45

It's about seven hours of pure story, or one or two days at a relaxed pace. The way XP is spread is deceptive:

| Range | Share of total XP |
|---|---|
| 1 to 20 | 0.2% |
| 20 to 30 | 2.5% |
| 30 to 40 | 27% |
| 40 to 45 | 70% |

Level 44 alone is a fifth of the total.

## Dailies and weeklies

There is no fixed list of dailies. Almost everything is entries that recharge and stack up to a cap. The rule is to never leave a stack full; playing three or four days a week, you lose nothing.

| Activity | Recharge | From |
|---|---|---|
| **Duty Quests** | 5 a day | 45 |
| **Conquest expeditions** | 10 cubes per week in each one (seen in Draupnir and Krao Cave) | 45 and item level 700 |
| **Nightmare** | 2 a day, stacks to 14 | 45 |
| **Transcendence** | 1 every 12 h, stacks to 14 | 45 and item level 1,600 |
| **Ascension Trial** | 3 per week | 45 and item level 1,000 |
| **Unknown Fissure** | 14 per week | 30 |
| Shugo Festival | 4 keys a day, stacks to 28 | |
| Sanctuary raids | 4 attempts per raid per week | 45 |
| Abyss Points | Weekly cap | 45 |

## At level 45

- What matters is total item level: 700, 1,400 and 2,100 for the three open Conquest tiers, and 2,800 for the first raid.
- Amulet and belt first: you don't replace them all season. The amulet is upgraded with the Monolith scrolls and the belt with the fortress ones.
- Everything to +10. According to the guides, it doesn't fail up to +10.
- No empty Arcana slot: a common card already adds 20. But cards come from Transcendence, at 1,600; at 45 it's normal not to have any.
- Each Daevanion crystal you spend adds 1 item level, permanently.
- There are 161 sealed dungeons, about ten minutes each. The ones of your faction give Kina, 2 Daevanion crystals, 2 Wisdom Stones and 1,250 enhancement stones.

## The class meta

Based on some 300,000 Korean combat logs:

| Content | On top |
|---|---|
| Dungeons | Assassin, Templar and Cleric |
| Arena | Gladiator and Assassin |
| Leveling and farming | Gladiator, Sorcerer and Spiritmaster |

The global version is on an older patch, so take it as a rough guide.

## What's coming

- **Ludra.** The 10-player raid NCSOFT removed on 7 October comes back with new mechanics; details by 16 October at the latest, according to the press. More in [the note on the 14 October patch](/en/apuntes/parche-14-oct-y-halloween/).
- **Season 2.** In Korea it brought new expeditions and raids, a Transcendence dungeon with ten difficulties, the middle Abyss and more Arcana slots.
- **Later, in Korea:** six stigma slots, stigmas up to level 25 and gathering locked at level 45 to fight bots.
- The client already has the names of things not yet open: three more Conquest tiers, three Transcendence dungeons and two raids.
