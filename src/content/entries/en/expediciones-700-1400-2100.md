## What people say

Guides talk about tiers going up 700 at a time for the Season 1 conquests, and put Transcendence at 1,600 or 2,400 depending on the source. What almost nobody says is whether the week's Kina is a single pot or goes per dungeon.

## What happens

This is what my Expedition menu, Conquest tab, showed on 12 October, at 1,519 item level:

| Dungeon | Stars | Item level | Players | Kina for killing every boss, per week | Guaranteed reward bar |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Krao Cave | 1 | 700 | 1 to 5 | 200,000 | 14 points |
| Draupnir | 1 | 700 | 1 to 5 | 200,000 | 14 points |
| Urugugu Canyon | 2 | 1,400 | **2 to 5** | 300,000 | 21 points |
| Vakron Sky Island | 2 | 1,400 | **2 to 5** | 300,000 | 21 points |
| Fire Temple | 3 | **2,100** | 1 to 5 | 400,000 | 28 points |
| Ferocious Horn Den | 3 | **2,100** | 1 to 5 | 400,000 | 28 points |

In Krao Cave, each run added **2 points** to the bar, so the 14-point one filled in 7 runs. I don't know if the 2 points come from the membership or apply to everyone. When you claim the reward, **the bar goes back to zero and a new one starts**: it doesn't run out like the week's Kina.

Each dungeon shows **its own things separately**: its Kina reward, its guaranteed bar and its “Remaining Unobtained Cube” 10/10. All the cubes are 40 Odyle Energy. Whether the ten cubes are per dungeon or shared I couldn't confirm: Krao Cave was still at 10/10 after two runs, although when I checked I had 0 energy. There's nothing at 1,600 in this menu. The 1,400 ones can't be done solo.

In Exploration, Ferocious Horn Den also requires 2,100, but it only gives 36,000 Kina for all the bosses.

What the rooms asked for in their titles, in combat power, that same day:

| Dungeons | Combat power asked |
|---|---|
| Krao Cave and Draupnir | 65k to 70k |
| Urugugu Canyon and Vakron Sky Island | 70k to 95k |
| Fire Temple | 95k to 105k, many with Cleric |
| Ferocious Horn Den | 100k to 115k |

At 1,519 item level and around 61k combat power, Krao Cave in Conquest already felt easy.

## What to do

- **Do every one you have unlocked, not just one.** Kina goes per dungeon: Krao Cave and Draupnir together give 400,000 a week, and with the 1,400 ones, 1,000,000.
- **The weekly Kina runs out; the guaranteed reward doesn't.** If a dungeon goes quickly for you, you can keep repeating it for the bar.
- **For Urugugu and Vakron, find a group**: they require at least two players.
- The next expedition wall is at **2,100**. How I got here is in [my notes from 45 to 1,000](/en/apuntes/del-45-a-los-1000/).
