## What people say

Korean guides give four keys a day with membership and a cap of 28, rounds at quarter past and quarter to the hour, and Wisdom Stones as the priority buy, which some English guides list at 350 tokens. They don't agree with each other on the keys or on the schedule.

## What happens

This is what my screen showed on 7 October at 22:00.

**The keys.** 3 recharge per day. The membership box says that with it you can store up to 21. A key is spent when you collect a reward, not when you enter, and the count is per server.

**The schedule.** The timeline runs on the hour. At 22:02 the 22:00 round was active, with all nine minigames open at once and a half-minute countdown to get in.

**The shop, consumables tab:**

| Item | Tokens | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Daevanion Crystal** | 50 | 10 per character |
| **Stigma Shard** | 300 | 35 per character |
| Soul Crystal | 50 | 30 a month per character |
| Empathy Candy | 20 | No limit shown |
| Radiant Odyle | 1,000 | 3 a month per character |

No Wisdom Stones and no Soul Codex. What it does have, and none of the guides I read listed it in this shop, is stigma shards.

The Daevanion Crystal has a “Step 1/4” label. My guess is that the price goes up in tiers as you buy, but I haven't bought any to check.

**The rest of the shop:**

| Tab | What's there | Tokens |
|---|---|---|
| Wings | Three | 2,100, 4,200 and 8,350 |
| Pets | Wandering Merchant Lugi | 3,600 |
| Paintings | Ten, for decoration | 2,000 each |
| Appearances | Individual pieces from three sets, earrings and necklaces | 530 to 5,400 |

**A minigame's rewards**, checked in Jump Jump. The token card gives 30 half the time, 40 30% of the time, 60 17% of the time and 100 3% of the time: about 40 on average. On top of that, there's a 20% chance of a piece of Rare gear.

## What to do

Spend tokens on Daevanion crystals first: at 50 they're the cheapest progress you can get, and there are only ten. Then stigma shards if you're short, which is normal while leveling. Cosmetics can wait.

Don't count on getting skill points from this shop, at least for now.

With three keys a day, the cap of 21 fills in a week. Use them in the games where you place high, and don't let them pile up to the limit.
