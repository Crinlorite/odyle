## What people say

The tables pulled from the global client give Plentiful Harvest three levels, with a 10, 20 and 30% chance of getting double material, and say that levels 2 and 3 require the Professional rank.

## What happens

It matches. With gathering at Professional 15, the skill in the Odyle branch shows as MAX at level 3, and its text says: “Upon a successful Essence Extraction, has a 30% chance to obtain double Gathered Materials”.

At Novice the cap was 1: at Novice 50, the three skills in the branch (Seeker, Plentiful Harvest and Appraiser) showed as MAX with one point each.

The points, counted on my panel:

| Rank | Common points earned | Specialty points earned |
|---|---|---|
| Novice 50 | 45 | 6 |
| Professional 15 | 60 | 7 |

At Professional the common skills go past 5: I had Proficient Handling at 7 and Delicate Touch at 6.

## What to do

In the branch of the resource you gather most, take Plentiful Harvest to 3 before touching Appraiser. The first gives you more material of everything; the second only rerolls the quality.

The full points plan is in [the note on gathering and crafting](/en/apuntes/recoleccion-y-oficios/).
