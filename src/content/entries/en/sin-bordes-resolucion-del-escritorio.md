## What people say

Nothing. I haven't seen it in any settings guide.

## What happens

With a 4K monitor, borderless mode always runs at 3840×2160. If you pick a lower resolution, the game switches you to windowed. I tried lowering it and went back.

The settings file kept the resolution from that attempt, but it isn't the one the game uses.

## What to do

If your graphics card can't handle your monitor's resolution, don't lower the resolution: lower FSR. The interface stays sharp and the world is rendered at a lower resolution underneath. With FSR on High Performance, medium quality, textures on high and a 45 frame limit, the game runs very comfortably on this machine.
