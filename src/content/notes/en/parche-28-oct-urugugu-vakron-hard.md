## The maintenance

The one on Wednesday 28 October 2026 started at 07:30, Spanish time, with four and a half hours planned: back around 12:00.

## Softer bosses in expeditions and Transcendence

NCSOFT lowers boss strength so fewer people die in one hit and the timer runs out less often.

| Where | What changes |
|---|---|
| Normal expeditions and Transcendence stage 1 | Boss Attack −15% |
| Hard expeditions and Transcendence stages 2 to 4 | Boss Attack and Damage Boost, −10% each |
| Transcendence stages 1 and 2 | Boss HP −6.5%, so it goes into Frenzy less often |

## Urugugu Canyon and Vakron Sky Island on Hard

After Krao Cave and Draupnir on 21 October, now it is the next two. Same rules:

- **Entry from item level 2,700.**
- The boss goes into Frenzy after **4 minutes**.
- **3 revives per player.**
- More Kina than Normal.

**Rewards:** Philosopher's Stone: Revelation (Bound), as a chance drop or as the guaranteed reward; Ariel Fragment: Urugugu and Ariel Fragment: Vakron, guaranteed; Expedition Hero Artwork, as a chance drop; and all Unique crafting materials (Wrathful Mind, Wrathful Will and Wrathful Ego). In Substance Morph, 100 Ariel Fragments turn into one Daevanion Crystal: Ariel, up to 30 times per character, or 1 fragment into 5 Enhance Stones, with no limit. There are new Hard achievements that give Unique skins.

## More HP for everyone at level 45

Base HP at level 45 goes up for every class, and more for the melee ones that take the boss head-on:

| Class | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Gladiator and Templar | 4,702 | **6,202** (+1,500) |
| Assassin, Ranger, Sorcerer, Spiritmaster, Chanter and Cleric | 4,702 | **5,702** (+1,000) |

**Assassin**, the only class with skill changes:

| Skill | Change |
|---|---|
| Heightened Sixth Sense | Gives more HP: 1,000 / 2,000 / 4,000 at levels 5, 10 and 20 |
| Illusive Clone | Cooldown from 90 to **60 seconds** |

## New daily dungeon: Odylium Repository

It gives Soul Crystals (Bound) based on the points you score. It **shares entries** with the Daeva Bio-Research Base daily: they are not new entries, just one more dungeon to spend them in.

## Shop: server transfers are over

The transfer period closes, and with it the Server Transfer tab in the shop. In Shop, Wind Breeze Merchants, Special:

| Item | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Odyle Energy (Bound) | 20 per character, in total | Removed. Two come in: **4 per character per week** and **16 per server per week** |
| Instant Clear Ticket: Nightmare (Bound) | Not there | **14 per server per week, at 30,000 Kina** |
| Instant Clear Ticket: Daily Dungeon (Bound) | A different limit | **14 per server per week, at 30,000 Kina** |
| Lower Abyss Rift Stone (7 days) | On sale | No longer sold |

Instant Clear Ticket: Daily Dungeon and Soul Crystal move from that tab to Substance Morph, Special, Wind Breeze Merchants.

## Crafted and expedition gear

- Crafted gear gets a higher Amplify cap: True Dragon and Star Dragon (Splendent too), from 1 to **3**; White Dragon and Dark Dragon (Splendent too), from 3 to **5**.
- You can now Transfer tier 3 expedition gear to tier 2 crafted gear, and tier 2 expedition gear to tier 1 crafted gear. NCSOFT's example: Enraged Kromede Necklace → White Dragon Lord Necklace.

## Season missions

Some weekly rewards change: Land Grabs no longer gives Abyss Points and gives Lower Abyss Rift Stone instead, and Watcher Kaira also gives Lower Abyss Rift Stone.

## The Abyss and matchmaking

- More people fit in the Abyss.
- The Lower Abyss Dimensional Core has more HP, but if the Artifact Battle lasts 10 minutes or more, it gets the Dissipation of Power debuff and weakens.
- **New matchmaking:** Nezekan (Elyos) goes against **Azphel (Asmodian)**. NCSOFT warns that pairings will change and that sometimes two servers of the same faction may be paired.

## Gathering and other improvements

- **Gathering needs level 45** (it was 10) and has a cap of **1,000 per day per server**, reset at 08:00. NCSOFT says it may change it.
- **Deleting a character takes 7 days**, it was 1, to stop the abuse of deleting and recreating.
- When idle, the game sends you back to the lobby after **30 minutes**, it was 3 hours.
- More Hidden Cubes spawn.

## “Atreia's Call” attendance event

28 days of login rewards, from today until the 2 December maintenance. The most useful: 2,000 Soul Crystals every seven days, 2 Odyle Energy on days 7, 14, 21 and 28, Resurrection Spiritstones, Battle Enhance Scrolls, two Content Usage Ticket Selection Chests and Unknown Fissure entries. It is in the menu, Attendance.

## Cash shop and pass

- New pass, **Daeva Pass: Prelude to Flight**, until 2 December. The basic one is free and the premium one costs 1,000 Quna. It mainly hands out Odyle Energy, Unknown Fissure and Nightmare entries and Resurrection Spiritstones, with a cap of 15,000 EXP per character per week.
- New skins until 11 November: the Vulpine Sunshower and No-Nonsense Maid sets, the Secret Teatime weapon for each class, the Azure Flame Kumiho and Glossy Pink Puppy pets, the Maid Ribbon Wings and helms and decor at 300 Quna.

## Fixes and refunds

- The revive counter did not always reset when retrying a Hard expedition.
- Ariel and Azphel were swapped in the character progression summary.
- Some characters were invisible in PvP.
- Some season missions did not progress after killing the boss.
- Supply Requests that asked for Sweet Balaur Berry are removed.
- **The entries that did not reset on 25 October in Europe are refunded.** And attendance rewards claimed twice because of a transfer are taken back.

## What it means for me

- **Hard Urugugu and Vakron are far off for me:** they need 2,700 and Gerlari is at a bit over 2,100. What does affect me already is the softer bosses in Transcendence and Normal expeditions.
- **Gerlari gains 1,500 HP.** The Gladiator gets one of the biggest increases, along with the Templar.
- **Good timing for my Assassin,** the third character I have just picked: Illusive Clone every 60 seconds instead of 90.
- **Careful with deleting characters:** it now takes a week. Better to be sure before creating one.
- **Gathering only at 45.** My alts will feel it while levelling, but Sendalari is already 45. And the 1,000 cap is per server, so it is shared between all my characters.
- **Nightmare and daily entries for Kina** are one more way to save time if you have spare Kina: 30,000 each, up to 14 a week.
- **In the Abyss we get Azphel.**
