## The maintenance

The one on Wednesday 21 October 2026 started at 08:30, Spanish time, with five and a half hours planned: back around 14:00. At 11:19 NCSOFT had not announced any delay. As with the previous ones, the safe bet is to try logging in.

## Krao Cave and Draupnir on Hard

Both expeditions now have a Hard mode.

- **Entry from item level 2,600.**
- The boss goes into Frenzy after **4 minutes**.
- **3 revives per player.**
- It gives more Kina than Normal mode.

**New rewards:**

- Philosopher's Stone: Revelation (Bound), as a chance drop, and it can also be picked as the guaranteed reward.
- Ariel Fragment (Bound). The amount depends on the dungeon.
- Expedition Hero Artwork, as a chance drop.
- **All Unique crafting materials**, including Wrathful Mind, Wrathful Will and Wrathful Ego.

**New achievements:** one for collecting Expedition Hero Artwork and several for Expedition (Hard), which give Unique skins.

## Ariel Fragment and the Ariel board

Ariel Fragments are exchanged in Substance Morph, Special tab:

| Exchange | Where | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| 100 Ariel Fragment → 1 Daevanion Crystal: Ariel (Bound) | Daevanion | 30 times per character |
| 1 Ariel Fragment → 5 Enhance Stones | Consumables | No limit |

Those crystals level a **new Daevanion board, Ariel**. How many points you can get is limited per dungeon. NCSOFT does not say which nodes it has.

## Resurrection Spiritstones for Kina

“Rise to the Challenge” event, from 21 October to the 16 December maintenance, which is when season 1 ends. In Shop, Wind Breeze Merchants:

| Step | Price | Contents | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 25,000 Kina | 30 Resurrection Spiritstone (Season 1) | Per server per week |
| 2 | 50,000 Kina | 30 Resurrection Spiritstone (Season 1) | Per server per week |

## Classes

Only fixes, no balance changes. Gladiator, Templar, Assassin, Ranger, Cleric and Spiritmaster are unchanged.

| Class | Skill | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Chanter | Spinning Strike | The visual effect did not display properly |
| Sorcerer | Cold Storm | With stacked Frostbite it did not deal the extra damage; Frostbite did not match the skill level; with specialization 1 it was applied once more after the effect ended |
| Sorcerer | Fire Wall | It dealt extra damage to targets without Embers |

## The Abyss and the Market

- **More players** fit in the Abyss. NCSOFT gives no number.
- More HP for the Lower Abyss Dimensional Core and for **all Abyss bosses**.
- Kaira and Argo have slightly bigger hitboxes.
- **Market:** the limit on selling gathered materials that arrived on 14 October is eased. Some blocked players can sell again; others stay as they were.

## Fixes

- The Empyrean Traces hidden by Halloween decorations at Dawn Legion Base and Zumion Village, in Verteron, are moved to reachable spots.
- “[Movement Controls]” appeared repeatedly in Settings, Key Settings.
- A title did something different from what its description said.

## What I have seen myself and what not

Nothing on this page is verified in my client yet: I wrote it during the maintenance, reading NCSOFT's notices. At item level 2,135, Hard mode is a long way off for me. What does affect me now is the Resurrection Spiritstone merchant and the bigger Abyss, and I will check them when I log in.
