## What people say

One English guide says Draupnir opens at 700 item level; another, at 900. metabot lists 700 for Exploration and Conquest and 2,400 for Transcendence. On whether Conquest is worth doing early, hardly anyone says anything.

## What happens

This is what my screen showed on 9 October, at 800 item level:

| | Draupnir, Exploration | Draupnir, Conquest | Krao Cave, Conquest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Requirement | Level 45 and **700** | Level 45 and **700** | Level 45 and **700** |
| Players | 1 to 5 | 1 to 5 | 1 to 5 |
| Kina for killing every boss (weekly reward) | 72,000 | **200,000** | **200,000** |
| Kina from Odyle Energy cubes | 60%, after 7 runs this week | 100% | 100% |
| Cube | 40 Odyle Energy | 40 | 40 |
| Guaranteed reward | Bar full with the 7 runs I had done | At 14 | At 14 |

In Conquest it also shows “Remaining Unobtained Cube Co… 10/10”: ten cubes a week. I got into Draupnir at 704, so the 900 was wrong.

The golden Bakarma pieces are Unique, level 54. I got two, the chest piece and the boots, in my first Exploration runs.

## What to do

- **Exploration** to learn the dungeon and fill its guaranteed reward.
- **Conquest as soon as you can**, because it pays almost triple the Kina. Better with a group that goes for every boss, because the Kina comes from killing them all. In the lobbies they're advertised as “all bosses” or “3boss”.
- Quick-match groups often fall apart after the first boss.
- What comes next, at 1,400 and 2,100, is in [this other entry](/en/cuaderno/expediciones-700-1400-2100/).
