## What people say

The guides say there are two rune slots, that each rune adds 40 and that they come from regional quest chests; for the Elyos, from Verteron. Going to +1 never fails, and above that the rune can break: some guides say from +2, others from +3.

## What happens

- The first rune took me from **959 to 999**: exactly 40.
- I got the second one from the **Nightmare shop**: the **Clash Rune Chest (Bound)** costs **500 Phantasmal Fragments** and contains **1 Clash Rune and 9 Clash Rune Enhance Scroll**. With it, from 992 to 1,032.
- The rune is a “Special Rune”, item level 40: +0.5% PvE damage and survivability, 100 penetration, +1% combat speed and +1% multi-hit.
- I took both to **+2 without either one breaking**: from 1,040 to 1,044, one point per “+”.

On 9 October the Nightmare shop also had: Daevanion crystals at 300 (50 per character), Stigma Shard at 1,500 (35 per character), Soul Codex at 400 (5 a week), Soul Codex: Reset at 8,000, Amplify Stone fragments at 500, wings, appearances and boss statues.

## What to do

If you're missing a rune slot, your first Nightmare purchase is the chest. Then Daevanion crystals. With the runes at +2 I stopped: the leftover scrolls aren't worth the risk of losing 40 in one go.
