## What it's for

Just one thing: crafting **Strong Leather** in Armorsmithing. Level 1 recipes take 2 and level 25 recipes take 20. If you don't craft armor, sell it, even though the NPC only pays 9 Kina each.

## Where it comes from

**According to metabot:** only monsters and one chest, the Shulak Loot Bundle, drop it. It doesn't come from Substance Morph, so Odyle Energy is no use for getting it.

- The highest chance is from **Furious Saursus**, in Night Dew Cave (Verteron): 62%.
- For farming it in bulk, metabot ranks the **Abyss Swarm** in Steel Titan's Grave, in Chaotic Middle Reshanta, first. Each one has under 5%, but there are 60 on the map.
