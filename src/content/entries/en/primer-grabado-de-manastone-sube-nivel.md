## What people say

One guide says each Manastone adds 1 to 4 item levels depending on how good the roll is; another, that each line adds 1 if it's gray, 2 if green and 3 if blue. Another gives 30,000 Kina per Soul Binding attempt. Several advise against spending Manastones on gear you're going to replace, because they're lost.

## What happens

The window opens with N and has five tabs: Growth, Manastone, Theostone, Soul Binding and Transfer.

**Manastone.** Each “Engrave” uses one stone and 2,000 Kina, and rerolls the lines. I engraved every equipped piece once and item level went from 726 to **800**. Then I re-engraved the sword several times looking for better lines, and the level didn't move, but the new lines came out the same color as the old ones: three gray and one green. Whether a better roll raises it, as the guides say, I haven't been able to see.

The “Auto Engrave” button engraves on its own until it meets a target. You choose grade, number of lines and stats. With the lowest grade it only lets you ask for one line; with “Superior or Higher”, two. The lines that can roll, with their range:

| Line | Range |
|---|---|
| Attack | 1 to 3 |
| Defense | 10 to 30 |
| Accuracy Bonus | 5 to 7 |
| Evasion Bonus | 5 to 7 |
| Critical Hit | 5 to 7 |
| Critical Hit Resist | 5 to 7 |
| Block | 7 to 10 |
| HP | 30 to 60 |
| MP | 15 to 30 |

**Soul Binding.** It raises the stats the piece already has toward their cap. Two attempts left the Bakarma chest piece at 88.6%. The next one asked for **59,000 Kina**, and on the boots 42,000, plus Soul Codex. After three attempts, item level was still 800.

## What to do

- **Engrave a Manastone on every new piece you put on**, even if it's just for a while: it raises item level for 2,000 Kina.
- Soul Binding improves stats, but not the level. Re-engraving only raises it if better-colored lines roll. Save both for pieces you'll keep for a while, and for when you have Kina to spare.
- Before extracting an old piece, check the Transfer tab.
