## What people say

Almost no guide mentions it. A Korean one advises putting at least one point into each resource “so it shows up on the minimap”, and a wiki page mentions a search ability for each node type.

## What happens

With zero specialization points, the minimap marks nothing: you have to spot nodes with your own eyes. When I put a point into the Odyle branch, Odyle started showing up on the map.

You earn one specialization point every ten mastery levels, so the first one comes at Novice 10.

## What to do

Put your first specialization point into the resource you most want to see. And if the map marks Odyle and you can't find it, look up: it floats.
