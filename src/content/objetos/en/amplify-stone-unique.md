## What it's for

When a **gold** piece (Unique grade) reaches +15, [Enhance Stones](/en/objetos/enhance-stone/) are no longer enough: to keep going you have to **amplify** it, and every step uses these stones. According to metabot's calculator, the extra levels depend on the piece's item level: 1 at 54, 3 at 70 and 5 at 86. The first step takes 600,000 Kina and 6 stones and succeeds 66% of the time; later steps drop to 20%, and each failure raises the chance of the next attempt a little. The table is in [the note on currencies and materials](/en/apuntes/monedas-materiales-y-contenidos/). Metabot counts 354 pieces that can be amplified.

**Blue** pieces can't be amplified. In my game they stayed at +10 and went no further.

## Where it comes from

**According to metabot**, only from Substance Morph, by combining [fragments](/en/objetos/amplify-stone-fragment-unique/): according to wikily's recipe table, **4 fragments and 2,500 Enhance Stones** per stone. Since this unbound version is tradable, you can also buy it on the market.
