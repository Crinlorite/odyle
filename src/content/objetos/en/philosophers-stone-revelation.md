## What it's for

It's the step that comes after Soul Binding. When a piece reaches **100%** binding (the one you raise with Sync, which is what weapons like the [Rupture Rod](/en/objetos/rupture-rod/) are for), Soul Fuse adds a new power to it.

**According to the guides:** each Soul Fuse costs **2 of these stones and 1,000,000 Kina**. It always succeeds, and the power doesn't carry over to another piece if you change gear.
