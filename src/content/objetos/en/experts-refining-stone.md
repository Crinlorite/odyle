## What it's for

It's the material for 40 recipes from level 15 to 25. The **Expert's Orichalcum** weapons in Blacksmithing take 5 each, and the **Wind Breeze** and **Chaser** bracelets in Handicrafting take 2.

**Seen in game:** in the inventory it's the **green orb**, the one that sits next to the grey [Refining Stone](/en/objetos/refining-stone/). It can be sold on the Market. Check what people are asking for it there before handing it to the NPC, who only pays 160.

## Where it drops

**According to metabot**, it always drops from the Hero bosses of Chaotic Lower Reshanta (server time):

| Boss | When it spawns |
|---|---|
| Watcher Kaira | Every 3 hours from 01:00 |
| Guardian Lord Nahma | Sundays and Fridays at 21:00 |
| Executor Tamasa and Executor Argo | Mondays, Thursdays and Saturdays at 21:30 |

It can also drop from the last boss of Krao Cave, Draupnir, Urugugu Canyon, Vakron Sky Island and Ferocious Horn Den, and from the Fire Temple cubes, but not every time.
