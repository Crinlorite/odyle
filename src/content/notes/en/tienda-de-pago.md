## What I've seen and what I haven't

Of this page, I've only verified three things in my client: that membership costs €12.75, what the two passes cost in Quna, and that the Market doesn't open until level 45 even if you pay for it. The last two have their own entries: [the two passes in numbers](/en/cuaderno/dos-pases-temporada-1/) and [the Market at level 45](/en/cuaderno/mercado-nivel-45/).

The rest is prices published on 4 October 2026 and guides' opinions. Prices change; check them in your shop before trusting this table.

## What's there

| What | Price | What it gives and whether it's worth it |
|---|---|---|
| **Special Quai Membership** | €12.75 for 30 days | Market, trading, remote storage and the Wind Breeze shop. **Yes**, but the Market doesn't open until 45 |
| **Long pass**, Dawn Legion | 1,500 Quna | According to the guides, 120,000 enhance stones versus 48,000 free, and 1,000,000 bound Kina. Runs until 16 December. **Yes** |
| **Short pass**, Planet's Call | 1,000 Quna | Small amounts per level. Ends 28 October. **Check it before paying** |
| **Inventory expansion** | Quna | More slots in the cube. It's the second purchase in the Korean order |
| **Character slot** | 1,000 Quna | Another character, with its own weekly limits. **Only if you'll play more than one** |
| **Starter Pack** | €16.99 | Starter bundle. **Skippable** |
| **Appearances** | €25.49 or 2,200 Quna | Cosmetic; according to the guides, owning them adds stats. Up to you |
| **Hidden Cube keys** | 50 Quna, three per day | Opening cubes. **No** |

## Quna

It's the paid currency. The bundles, according to the 4 October analysis:

| Quna | Price |
|---|---|
| 400 | €5.09 |
| 1,000 | €12.75 |
| 2,000 | €25.49 |
| 4,000 | €50.99 |

The price per unit is the same in every bundle: buying the big one isn't cheaper.

## Exchanging Quna for Kina

You can, and the guides agree you shouldn't. The analysis puts it at €5.40 to €7.65 per million Kina, and freshly bought Quna is locked for 72 hours before you can exchange it.

## The Wind Breeze shop

It comes with membership and you pay with Kina. What the guides say to buy every week: 4 Odyle Energy at 100,000 Kina and 7 instant clear tickets at 30,000 Kina.

## My budget

Membership and passes, nothing more. It's my decision, not advice: that opens everything the game hides behind the shop without getting into what you buy with money to go faster.
