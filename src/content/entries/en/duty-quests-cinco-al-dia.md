## What people say

Guides rank them as the best return per minute in the daily routine, and Korean players advise checking the reward before accepting them.

## What happens

They're in the quest log (J), Duty tab, Mission sub-tab. This is what my screen showed on 10 and 11 October:

- **“Per Server Daily Completion 0/5”**: five a day, shared between the characters on the server.
- You can have up to 20 accepted at once.
- **Daily Refresh** rerolls the list for 1,000 Kina, up to 10 times a day.
- A list of about 13–15 quests of level 45 to 47, most of them common and the odd rare or epic one (“Wanted”, against an elite boss).
- **They all give the same base reward**: 50,000 Kina and 1,000 of another currency.
- On top of that, **1 to 5 random rewards from each quest's own list**: enhancement stones from 100 to 5,000, Kina from 6,000 to 100,000, crystals, materials or the odd piece of gear.

## What to do

Before accepting, open each quest and check its list. If you're short on enhancement stones, pick the ones that carry them in quantity; if you're short on Kina, the 80,000–100,000 ones. The “Wanted” ones cost more (an elite boss) and don't always pay better.
