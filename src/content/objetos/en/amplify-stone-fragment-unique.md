## What it's for

Just one thing: combining them in Substance Morph (“Conversión” in the Spanish client) to get an [Amplify Stone (Unique)](/en/objetos/amplify-stone-unique/). I haven't checked yet how many fragments each stone takes.

## Where it drops

**Seen in game:** Ascension Trial on Normal gave me **9** in one run, on 12 October.

**According to metabot:**

- The Abyssal Forge: Ludra boss always drops it.
- Fire Temple cubes, almost half the time (48.8%).
- The Deus Research Base bosses, one time in five.
- The Trade Shop and the Season Shop sell it for 500 Phantasmal Fragment.
