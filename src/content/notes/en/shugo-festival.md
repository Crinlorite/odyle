## What it is

Three- to five-minute minigames against other players where your gear and level don't count: races, memory, hide-and-seek, platform jumping. They're the light side of the game, and they still give Daevanion crystals and stigma shards, which are among the scarcest things while you level.

They don't need a high level: according to guides they open early, and rewards come in nine brackets by character level, starting at 10.

## If you're in a hurry, the essentials

1. **Join whenever it's convenient.** Entry is free, and just finishing gives tokens and enhancement stones.
2. **The key is spent when you claim the reward, not when you enter.** If you finished near the bottom, close the results without picking anything and you keep it.
3. **Don't leave your keys at the cap.** Keys that arrive with the counter full are lost.
4. **With tokens, Daevanion crystals first and then stigma shards.** Cosmetics last.

## The nine minigames

| Minigame | Length | Players | What it's about |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Jump Jump** | 2:55 | 1 to 4 | Dodge lasers and collect balloons. Touching a laser costs points |
| **Not This Tile?** | 3:15 | 1 to 6 | Memory: remember the safe tiles over eight rounds |
| **Hidden Lugi** | 3:30 | 2 to 9 | Hide-and-seek: some players search while the others, in disguise, collect Odyle |
| **Odyle Flight Frenzy** | 3:30 | 1 to 8 | Fly through a canyon collecting Odyle drops through the rings |
| **Goldrin's Treasure** | 3:50 | 1 to 8 | Find and open the chests marked by a beam of light |
| **Wraith Evasion** | 5:00 | 1 to 6 | Tag: if you get caught, you become a chaser |
| **Mysterious Track** | 5:00 | 1 to 8 | Mounted race with obstacles and boosts |
| **Defend Shugo Merchants** | 5:00 | 1 to 8 | Hold out against waves while protecting the merchants |
| **Up! Up! Up!** | 5:00 | 1 to 8 | Climb platforms. You only get a reward if you reach the first checkpoint |

Each time slot features several of the nine, not all of them.

## When it runs

On the hour. Guides talked about quarter past and quarter to, but on my screen the timeline goes by the hour, and at 22:02 the 22:00 round was active, with all nine minigames open at once. Next to the minimap, the Shugo icon shows up with the countdown.

## What you get

| Reward | How |
|---|---|
| **Centuryroot Tokens and enhancement stones** | Just for finishing |
| **Reward cards** | One key per card. The better you place, the more cards you can pick |

What I saw in Jump Jump's reward list: the token card gives 30 half the time, 40 30% of the time, 60 17% and 100 3%, about 40 on average. And there's a 20% chance of a Rare gear piece.

According to guides, the amounts go up with character level.

## The keys

- **Three a day.** That's what my client shows, not the four from the Korean guides.
- **With the membership you can store up to 21.** The box on the screen says so itself.
- **They're deducted when you claim a reward** and recharge on their own once a day. The count is per server.
- They can't be bought or farmed.

## The festival shop

You pay with Centuryroot Tokens. These are the prices on my screen on 7 October 2026; I go through them in detail in [the notebook entry](/en/cuaderno/tienda-shugo-festival/).

| Item | Tokens | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Daevanion Crystal** | 50 | 10 per character |
| **Stigma Shard** | 300 | 35 per character |
| Soul Crystal | 50 | 30 a month |
| Empathy Candy | 20 | No limit in sight |
| Radiant Odyle | 1,000 | 3 a month |
| Wings | 2,100, 4,200 and 8,350 | One of each |
| Wandering Merchant Lugi pet | 3,600 | One |
| Decorative paintings | 2,000 each | One of each |
| Appearances | 530 to 5,400 | One of each |

- **It doesn't sell Wisdom Stones or Soul Codex**, which is what the guides listed as the priority buy. It does sell stigma shards.
- The Daevanion crystal has a “Step 1/4” tag: the price seems to go up in steps. I haven't checked it by buying.
- One guide mentions a second, cheaper merchant that takes the same tokens. I haven't found it.

## Is it worth it?

Yes. A match takes five minutes, gives a reward even if you lose, and tokens buy Daevanion points and stigma shards, which you never have too many of while leveling. The only things to watch are not wasting keys on bad matches and not letting them fill up.
